In Seoul, people leave a laptop on the cafe table and walk out for twenty minutes.

Everyone assumes it's the CCTV. It isn't. Seoul has about 24 public cameras per 1,000 people — China has 494, twenty times more, and nobody there is holding a table with a MacBook. Cameras change the odds of getting caught. They don't change what crossed your mind.

The number that actually explains it: 75.7% of cash lost on the Seoul subway went back to the person who dropped it. Cash is untraceable. No camera makes you do that.
