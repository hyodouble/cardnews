## 확인이 필요한 사실

1. Seoul 24.28 and London 13.4 public CCTV cameras per 1,000 people come from Comparitech's most-surveilled-cities study, updated 25 June 2025. It counts government-operated cameras, and the study itself warns some private cameras may be mixed in for certain cities, so the two figures are not perfectly like-for-like.
2. The 494.25 cameras per 1,000 people figure for China is a nationwide estimate from the same Comparitech study, not a single-city count. City-level figures there run from about 56 (Beijing) to 120 (Taiyuan) per 1,000 — all still far above Seoul, so the 20x framing holds either way.
3. Seoul subway cash figures (10,082 finds / 581 million won; 7,630 returned / 440 million won; 75.7%) are Seoul Metro numbers reported in Korean press. Confirm the exact reference year before publishing. The overall return rate across all lost-item types is 51.4% (86,224 of 167,738), much lower than the cash rate — do not let the caption imply 75.7% applies to everything.
4. Unmanned-store theft counts (3,514 in 2021; 6,018 in 2022; 10,847 in 2023) are National Police Agency figures cited in Korean press. The number of unmanned stores roughly doubled in three years to about 12,000 by end-2025, so this is a raw count, not a per-store crime rate.
5. The 'ask a stranger to watch your bag' line is a widely reported everyday norm, not a measured statistic. It is written as an observation. Slide 9 exists specifically so the carousel cannot be read as 'nothing gets stolen in Korea' — do not cut it for length.
6. China comparison is about camera density only. No claim is made about Chinese crime rates or Chinese people's behavior, and none should be added in the caption or comment replies.

**03. You'll say it's **the cameras****
  EN  Fair guess. Seoul really is heavily surveilled — about 24 public CCTV cameras per 1,000 people, roughly double London's 13. Koreans know they're on camera almost everywhere.
  KO  그럴듯하다. 서울은 실제로 감시가 촘촘하다. 공공 CCTV가 1,000명당 약 24대로, 런던의 13대보다 두 배 가까이 많다. 한국인은 어디서든 찍히고 있다는 걸 안다.

**04. But China has **twenty times more****
  EN  China averages roughly 494 cameras per 1,000 people — about 20x Seoul. If cameras produced trust, the most surveilled country on earth would be the most trusting one. It isn't.

  KO  카메라는 잡힐 확률을 바꿀 뿐, 그 노트북을 집어갈 생각이 드는지 여부는 못 바꾼다. 한국에서 대다수에게 그건 애초에 선택지가 아니다.
