## 확인이 필요한 사실

1. 117,000 won (2025 nationwide average) and 134,000 won (Seoul) come from NH NongHyup Bank's analysis of 5.33 million wedding-gift transfers, 2023-2025, reported by the Korea Herald. Bank-transfer data skews toward younger, urban senders; cash-only envelopes aren't captured, so the true average may differ.
2. The '60% say 100,000 won is appropriate' figure is from an Incruit survey of 844 office workers (May 2025) about a coworker's wedding specifically. It is opinion, not behavior, and the same survey found casual, work-only coworkers still lean toward 50,000 won.
3. 85,000 won average wedding meal cost is a Korea Consumer Agency (한국소비자원) figure from April 2025; it varies a lot by venue tier (hotel vs. standard wedding hall).
4. The odd-number/no-multiples-of-4 rule is a widely followed folk custom, not a written law, and 100,000 won is treated as an exception despite being an even count of 10,000s. Younger urban guests increasingly default to round numbers like 100,000 regardless.

**02. It's called **chukuigeum****
  EN  Chukuigeum (축의금) — congratulatory money. Cash in a white envelope, handed in at a front desk before the ceremony, your name written on the outside.

**03. 50,000 won **used to be enough****
  EN  For a casual coworker or distant acquaintance, 50,000 won (about $35) was the standard for years. In 2025 that norm is losing ground fast.
  KO  그냥 아는 사이나 먼 직장 동료라면 오랫동안 5만 원이 기준이었다. 2025년 들어 그 기준이 빠르게 무너지고 있다.

**04. The new floor is **100,000 won****
  EN  Bank transfer data shows 100,000-won gifts overtaking 50,000-won ones nationwide. A 2025 survey found 60% of office workers now call 100,000 the right amount for a coworker's wedding.
  KO  은행 이체 데이터를 보면 전국적으로 10만 원이 5만 원을 앞지르고 있다. 2025년 설문에서 직장인 60%가 동료 결혼식엔 10만 원이 적당하다고 답했다.

**05. The amount **can't be even****
  EN  Gifts are almost always odd — 30,000, 50,000, 70,000 — never 40,000 or 60,000. Odd numbers are considered lucky; even ones are read as bad luck for a couple starting a life together.
  KO  3만, 5만, 7만처럼 홀수로 낸다. 4만, 6만은 쓰지 않는다. 홀수는 길한 수로, 짝수는 신혼부부에게 좋지 않은 숫자로 여겨진다.

**06. In most countries, gifts are **private****
  EN  In Korea the amount is written down, tied to your name, and remembered. Give too little and it gets noticed. This works like a ledger between families as much as a present.
  KO  한국에선 액수가 이름과 함께 기록되고 기억된다. 적게 내면 알아챈다. 선물이라기보다 가족과 지인 사이의 장부에 가깝다.

**07. It's also **built to be repaid****
  EN  When that guest gets married later, the couple is expected to give back roughly the same amount — sometimes more, adjusted for how much prices have risen since.
  KO  그 하객이 나중에 결혼하면, 신랑신부는 그때 받았던 만큼, 혹은 물가가 오른 만큼 더해서 돌려준다.

**08. 117,000 — won is the **average wedding cash gift** in Korea now**
  EN  Bank data across 5.3 million transfers puts the 2025 nationwide average at 117,000 won ($85), and 134,000 won in Seoul — up from 110,000 in 2023.
  KO  530만 건의 계좌이체 데이터를 분석한 결과 2025년 전국 평균은 11만 7천 원(약 85달러), 서울은 13만 4천 원이었다. 2023년 11만 원에서 올랐다.

**09. The math **isn't random****
